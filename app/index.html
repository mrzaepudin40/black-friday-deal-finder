How these deals were checked

Every price on this page was read by our agent on 27 Nov 2026 from a deals source that names the store and shows both the sale price and the usual price. The discount is computed from those two prices, never from a store's own claim. NEW LOW is used only when a source says so in words; STRONG DEAL means 30%+ off; SOLID DEAL means 20–29% off; ORDINARY SALE means under 20% off — a modest cut, flagged honestly.

Prices change fast on Black Friday: always confirm the price at the store before you buy. Store links go to the store's own search for the exact product. FridayLens is an independent deal finder, not affiliated with any store named here.
